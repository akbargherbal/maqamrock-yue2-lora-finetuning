The sharp bit. Because the cap sits only 7 frames above the guide's natural plan, any arm that inserts a section repeat overruns 7750 and reports yue2.semantic.truncated 1. That makes the truncation flag a cheap, objective repeat detector — no audio required.

4 · What the screen can and cannot tell us

Can
  • planned length per arm (frames / 25) vs the guide's 309.7 s
  • whether the plan overran the cap (truncated, the model's own flag)
  • AR wall cost per arm (yue2.semantic_ms)
  • rank the 16 arms before spending NAR time on them
Cannot
  • say which line was sung — semantic.json is codec indices with no lyric↔frame alignment
  • prove 2× vs 3× on the outro as such; only that the plan got longer
  • replace listening — the verbatim verdict needs the rendered audio, or an ASR-vs-sheet comparison that does not exist yet

5 · Three things called "ABC" — keep them apart

1 · ABC extractiondone yesterday

Audio → score.abc via SheetSage2. 12 files sit on GCS under out/abc_v2/. Today we only downloaded one. None are being generated now.

2 · The semantic screenrunning

stop_after=semantic → semantic.json. Because that option is documented as "ABC planner + semantic AR", the two cot=melody arms without abc_file also drop a score.abc of the model's own plan — a byproduct, and not the same file as an abc_v2/ transcription.

3 · ABC as inputc1 / c2 / c3

The guide's abc_v2 score.abc, passed through abc_file, steers the melody — not the words.
